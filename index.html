What is a snake plant

Snake plants (Dracaena trifasciata) are widely known as one of the most forgiving houseplants. They tolerate missed waterings, prefer dry soil, and grow steadily at their own pace. This makes them a gentle choice for beginners and for anyone who wants something living without high demands.

A calm bedroom companion. Snake plants use a night-time photosynthesis process (CAM), taking in carbon dioxide at night. Many people like them in bedrooms for this quiet rhythm. It is not medical advice, just one small way to bring nature into a resting space.

A cleaner start. Many fully grown plants are shipped in dense soil that can hide pests or arrive stressed from transit. Our kits begin with cured cuttings, prepared by hand in small batches, with minimal soil and a fresh start.

Quick care notes

Snake plants prefer bright, indirect light and dry soil. In summer, avoid harsh direct sun. In winter (UK), place closer to a window for extra daylight. Water sparingly. Less is usually better.

If you choose water propagation, keep the cut end just submerged and refresh the water when it becomes cloudy or about once per week. Once roots form, you can plant into soil when you are ready.
